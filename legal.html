Your data & privacy

All processing happens locally in your browser. The job posting and resume text you paste are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any ResumeChkr server — we don't have one, and no analytics on this site ever sees the content of your resume or job posting. Closing or reloading this page clears the text in the boxes above. The one thing that does persist is your scan history — the posting's title line, the date, and your score, saved to this browser's local storage so you can track what you've checked and applied to; it stays on this device and is never transmitted anywhere, and you can clear it anytime from the "Your scan history" section. We don't set up user accounts. We do use Google Analytics to see aggregate traffic (pages visited, general location, device type) — it sets standard analytics cookies and works the same way it does on most websites, but it never receives the text you paste into the boxes above or your scan history, and it only runs if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner (or the "Cookie Settings" button in the corner) — reject it and no analytics cookies are set. The one exception to the "nothing leaves your device" rule is the optional "Use your own AI" section: if you choose to copy the generated prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or another AI, that text goes to whichever service you pasted it into, subject to that service's own privacy policy — that's a step you take yourself, not something ResumeChkr sends on your behalf.
